# Little City Adventures · Community Edition

An open-world Unity game with six procedural 3D explorers. Walk, ride bikes, skate, drive and sail around the city and its islands. Choose Original, Bubbly or Living Glass in Controls.

Open the project in Unity 6000.3.12f1, open `Assets/LittleCity/Scenes/LittleCity.unity`, and press Play. The character selector renders its portraits from the same models used in the world.
